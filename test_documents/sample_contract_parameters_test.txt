EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 by and between Northwind Legal Group, a Delaware corporation (
"Company"), and Alex Morgan ("Employee").

1. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Employee shall serve as Senior Counsel and shall perform responsibilities related to contract review, compliance analysis, software licensing review, and employment policy guidance.

2. COMPENSATION
Employee shall receive an annual base salary of $180,000. Employee may also be eligible for annual performance bonus based on company objectives and legal risk review performance.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee shall maintain the confidentiality of all non-public company information, including customer contracts, product roadmap details, litigation materials, and internal compliance reports.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Any work product created during employment, including legal analyses, templates, contract drafts, and internal research materials, shall be assigned to the Company.

5. NON-COMPETE
Employee shall not work for a direct competitor of the Company in the United States for a period of twelve (12) months following termination.

6. TERMINATION
The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately for cause, including material misconduct, unauthorized disclosure of confidential information, or failure to comply with company policies.

7. GROUNDS FOR DISPUTE
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved under the laws of the State of New York, excluding any conflict of law principles.

8. INDEMNIFICATION
The Company shall indemnify Employee for claims arising from actions taken in good faith within the scope of employment, except where gross negligence or intentional misconduct is established.

9. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY
The Company is committed to equal opportunity employment and prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, age, or veteran status.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rule.

11. DATA PROTECTION
Employee shall ensure that data handled in connection with this Agreement is protected in accordance with applicable privacy law and internal information security policies.

12. ACCEPTANCE
The parties acknowledge that they have read this Agreement and agree to be bound by its terms.
